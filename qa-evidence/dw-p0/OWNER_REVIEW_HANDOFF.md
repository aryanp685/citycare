## Decision resolution: who reviews what

- **Owner / privacy / security:** confirm policy and residual risk for authorization and expiry, verification and account/session boundaries, field-level scopes and demographics, documents, post-withdrawal metadata and independent purposes, consent history, clock rollback, audit/erasure and emergency care (see A-routed IDs in the tracker and all relevant scope IDs). These cannot be settled by a passing technical test.
- **Qualified clinical review:** primary clinical decisions DW-OD-10–14 and DW-OD-16; clinical input is also required on care-impacting authorization, scope, emergency, audit and release cards as listed below. Clinical review does not grant privacy permission; owner/privacy separately approves disclosure.
- **Engineering validation:** technical-primary DW-OD-04, 05, 07, 24, 27, 31, 32, 41–43, 48. Validate cross-writer synchronization, absence/replacement and erasure races, FK/index/trigger and ledger order, scope predicates and output leakage, fresh expiry time, commit uncertainty, actual transaction/pooler compatibility, bounded waits and isolated test barriers. Measurements and lock mechanics are engineering choices; policy, migration authorization and accepted residual risk are not inferred from tests. The source register retains all 48 owner approvals as PENDING even where technical work is primary.
- **Release owner:** DW-OD-46 is a separate go/no-go after applicable approvals, implementation authorization and evidence. Technical recommendation, qualified clinical assessment, policy approval and release acceptance are distinct.
